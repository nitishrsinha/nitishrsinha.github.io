Pragmatic DML with AI-Learned Representations

Andres Aradillas Fernandez, Victor Chernozhukov, Carlos Cinelli, Sven Klaassen et al. · 2026-10-01

Text, images, and other rich covariates are increasingly compressed into AI-learned representations and then used as controls in causal analysis. We study when this approach is valid and develop a practical framework for causal inference with learned representations.

Modeling Shipping Emissions: Machine Learning, Engineering, and Policy Counterfactuals

Hiroyuki Kasahara, Allen Peters, Oliver Xu · 2026-10-01

Machine learning predicts outcomes well, but predictive accuracy does not ensure reliable counterfactual responses. We examine how to combine machine learning and theory for measurement and counterfactual analysis, using maritime CO2 emissions where physics provides a benchmark speed response.

Lorenzo Cardarelli · 2026-10-01

The study of ceramic materials constitutes a cornerstone of archaeological research, yet the post-production workflow for pottery documentation remains labor-intensive and creates significant publication bottlenecks. This paper presents PyPottery, an open-source, AI-powered suite designed to semi-automate the complete ceramic documentation pipeline.
